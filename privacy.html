Privacy Policy

Pepites GTA Bot – last updated November 20, 2026

1. Data we access

When you connect TikTok, we receive, with your permission: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, and the ability to publish videos you confirm through the video.publish scope.

2. How we use it

Your profile information is only shown on the publish screen so you can see which account you are posting to. The access token is used only to upload the clips you confirm. We do not read your private messages, followers, likes or any other data.

3. Storage

The access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the computer running the tool, in a file readable only by the owner. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and no analytics or advertising trackers are used.

4. Deleting your data

You can revoke access at any time from TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data. You can also ask us to delete it by email.

5. Children

The tool is not intended for people under 18.
